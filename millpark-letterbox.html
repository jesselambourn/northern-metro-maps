How maps are ranked. Each enrolled home (every unit counted separately) gets a modelled chance of being a supporter, from the Northern Metro doorknock model (household age, size, ancestry, units) adjusted for the local Victorian Socialists and Labor-vs-Liberal vote. Only 111 Mill Park doors have been knocked, so this is model only. Score = likely supporters per 100 enrolled homes × (enrolled share of letterboxes)0.25, so unenrolled letterboxes only count lightly.

How clusters are formed. Start from the best map not yet used, add its best touching neighbour until there are 3, then keep adding neighbours from the top third of maps (up to 6). Leftover pairs and singles join the best cluster they touch if they are in the top third themselves; weaker leftovers are left out. Clusters are listed by score.
