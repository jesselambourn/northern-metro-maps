With no district chosen, each centre shows its rank within the district it belongs to. Support compares voters with the average of whatever you rank by (all NM, inner or outer NM, or the district): Weak (more than 10% below), Below average, Average (up to 10% above), Good (10–20% above), Strong (20–30% above), Very strong (30%+ above). With past VS vote On (default), support is half the actual VS vote in voters' areas (2022 state Lower House, 2022 state Upper House, 2025 Senate and, in Cooper and Scullin where VS ran, the 2025 House vote; whichever of these is strongest in the area counts double) and half our doorknock model (demographics, nudged by the same past VS votes and the 2022 Labor vs Liberal vote), each compared with the Northern Metro average. With it Off, support is the doorknock demographic model alone (with the Labor vs Liberal nudge, no VS vote). Booth popups always show the past VS vote at that booth, or in its voters' area where it's new or small. How to use this page

This page shows which early voting centres (EVCs) and election-day booths matter most for VS in Northern Metropolitan.

How to use it

  1. Switch between "Early voting centres" and "Election day booths" with the buttons.
  2. "Past VS vote" switches the 2022 state and 2025 federal VS results in or out of the support figures. Off uses our doorknock model only.
  3. "All NM" ranks every centre for the whole region. "Inner / Outer" ranks within outer NM (Broadmeadows, Greenvale, Kalkallo, Thomastown) or inner NM (the other 7 districts). "Per district" ranks centres for one district: choose it from the menu, or click it on the map.
  4. Each circle is a voting centre. The number is its priority (1 = most important). Click a circle, or a name in the list, to see its name, address and details.

What the circles mean

  • Size: how many votes we expect at the centre in total.
  • Colour: how likely voters there are to support VS, from Weak (pale) to Very strong (dark). It's compared with the average of whatever you're ranking by: all of Northern Metro, inner or outer NM, or the district. So the best and worst centres stand out within each.
  • Dashed ring: another district also gets a big share of its votes.
  • Square (per district view): the centre belongs to a neighbouring district, but enough of this district's voters go there that it's on the list.
  • Faded: not on the chosen district's list.

How the priority is worked out

The ranking multiplies how many Northern Metro voters (or the chosen district's voters) will vote at each centre by how likely they are to support VS, counted twice (squared). Support is half the actual VS vote in the area at the 2022 state election (Lower and Upper House) and 2025 federal election (Senate, and the House where VS ran), and half our doorknock model. Bigger, friendlier centres come first.

The centres shown for each district follow the VEC's own lists, leaving out any where the district's voters cast less than 10% of the votes.

For shared centres, please talk to the neighbouring district's coordinator so you can split the roster and make sure every shift is covered.
